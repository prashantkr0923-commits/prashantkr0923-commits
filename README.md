### Product work

**[Job-Ready English: a voice AI tutor](https://github.com/prashantkr0923-commits/job-ready-english-voice-tutor)** · [try it](https://elevenlabs.io/app/talk-to?agent_id=agent_8601m5x7xma4e4tr0ke8bqm1m5s7)
A voice tutor that teaches entry-level job seekers in India the English words their target job uses, in 5-minute sessions explained in Hindi or English. Each word climbs a three-step ladder, from repeating a sentence to making an original one, after testing showed that asking a nervous speaker for a sentence straight away was where sessions stalled. Built on ElevenLabs Agents with Claude, and refined over five prompt versions against a 19-test plan.

**[Quote Copilot: an AI procurement agent](https://github.com/prashantkr0923-commits/quote-copilot-procurement-agent)** · [live demo](https://appapppy-cl7jquf8iannuwn4kvf32r.streamlit.app/) (password on request)
Reads messy vendor quotations (spreadsheets, PDFs, Word letters, emails and phone photos), turns them into one comparison a buyer can trust, and answers award questions in plain English. The AI reads and code calculates, so every number traces back to the vendor's own words. Matched all 207 quoted lines within 1% on a test set with 38 planted problems.

**[LinkedIn newsletter digest agent](https://github.com/prashantkr0923-commits/linkedin-newsletter-digest-agent)** *(in development)*
Turns a week of LinkedIn newsletter emails into one ranked Sunday digest that learns from Worth it, Read later and Not interested marks. A hand-marked sample showed the sending newsletter predicts interest better than the topic, and in testing 70% of the ranking's top 10 were articles the reader liked, against 44% for newest first. Built on Google Apps Script and the Claude API, with an open-source release planned.

**[Personal finance agent](https://github.com/prashantkr0923-commits/personal-finance-agent)**
Turns bank transaction alerts and statements into one deduplicated ledger, reconciles it against the statement, classifies spending and sends a scheduled report. An immutable data model means every fix rebuilds the history from raw input. A natural-language query agent is next, with its evaluation harness built first.
